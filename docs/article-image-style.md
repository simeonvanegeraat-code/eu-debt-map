# EU Debt Map editorial image style

Use this style for new articles and for articles that receive a substantive
editorial update. Existing images are replaced in small reviewed batches, not
all at once.

## Visual language

- Use a polished isometric 3D editorial illustration on a deep navy background.
- Make a recognisable European or country silhouette the main subject.
- Use cool white and silver materials, EU blue highlights and restrained warm
  gold accents for connections or emphasis.
- Keep the composition calm and readable at card size, with one clear focal
  subject and generous space around it.
- Use soft cinematic lighting, subtle depth and clean financial-institution
  symbols when they clarify the topic.

## Consistency and accuracy

- Prefer a 16:9 landscape crop, at least 1,200 pixels wide.
- Do not put headlines, numbers, labels, flags, logos or watermarks in the image.
- Do not render banknote text, official seals or invented chart values.
- Avoid crisis imagery, flames, explosions, distressed people and exaggerated
  red warning graphics unless the article is specifically about a documented
  crisis.
- The image should illustrate the subject; exact figures stay in HTML charts and
  article text where they can be sourced and updated.

